# Month-end close checklist: restaurant

Work top to bottom. A restaurant deposits several kinds of money (cards, cash,
delivery app payouts, tips), so the sales tie-out in step 3 has to be right
before anything below it.

Month: ____________  Closed by: ____________  Date locked: ____________

## 1. Get every statement and report in
- [ ] Bank statements for every account, as PDFs, for the full calendar month
- [ ] Credit card statements (the one whose closing date falls inside the month)
- [ ] Point-of-sale monthly sales report, with tax, tips, discounts and voids
- [ ] Payout reports from the card processor and each delivery app
- [ ] Payroll register for every pay run dated in the month, with tip reports

## 2. Reconcile each bank and card account to the printed closing balance
- [ ] Ledger balance equals the closing balance printed on the statement, after
      uncleared checks and deposits in transit
- [ ] Card: previous balance + charges + fees + interest - payments - credits
      = printed new balance

## 3. Tie sales to deposits
- [ ] POS total sales (food, beverage, tax, tips) equals card batches plus cash
      plus gift card redemptions plus delivery app sales
- [ ] Each processor and delivery app payout equals its gross sales less fees,
      commissions and refunds; the fees are expenses, not reductions of the
      deposit
- [ ] Cash over and short is a separate line, and large amounts are explained

## 4. Pair transfers
- [ ] Transfers between your own accounts appear on both statements
- [ ] Card payments from checking are recorded as transfers, not expenses

## 5. Tips and sales tax held for others
- [ ] Tips collected on cards equal tips paid out to staff plus tips still
      owed; the balance owed is a liability, not income
- [ ] Sales tax collected per POS, less tax remitted, equals sales tax payable

## 6. Vendors and payables
- [ ] Food, beverage and supply bills dated in the month entered, paid or not
- [ ] Payables aging checked against vendor statements for the main suppliers

## 7. Inventory and cost of sales
- [ ] Food and beverage counts taken on the last day of the month
- [ ] Cost of sales equals opening inventory + purchases - closing inventory
- [ ] Food cost percentage reviewed against the target

## 8. Payroll from the register
- [ ] Gross wages, employer taxes and withholdings entered from the register
- [ ] Wages split between kitchen and front of house if you track labor cost
      by area

## 9. Other adjustments
- [ ] Prepaids (insurance, annual licences) and depreciation posted
- [ ] Loan interest split from principal; loan balance agrees with the lender

## 10. Review and lock
- [ ] P&L reviewed against last month: sales, food cost, labor and rent as
      percentages of sales
- [ ] Balance sheet scanned for negative balances and uncategorized items
- [ ] Month locked and reports sent
