# Month-end checklists

Month-end close checklists for small businesses and the bookkeepers who close
their books. Each folder holds a checklist in Markdown, in the order the work
happens, and a CSV tracker with one row per step that you can copy into a
spreadsheet each month.

| Folder | For |
|---|---|
| `small-business/` | A small business with a few bank and card accounts, closing its own books or with a bookkeeper |

Maintained by [ZeraBooks](https://www.zerabooks.com).
