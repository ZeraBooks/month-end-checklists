# Month-end close checklist: small business

Work top to bottom. Each step depends on the ones above it, so adjusting
entries made before the bank accounts tie usually have to be redone.

Month: ____________  Closed by: ____________  Date locked: ____________

## 1. Get every statement in
- [ ] Bank statements for every account, as PDFs, for the full calendar month
- [ ] Credit card statements (the one whose closing date falls inside the month)
- [ ] Loan and merchant processor statements
- [ ] Payroll register for every pay run dated in the month

## 2. Reconcile each bank and card account to the printed closing balance
- [ ] Ledger balance equals the closing balance printed on the statement, after
      uncleared checks and deposits in transit
- [ ] Card: previous balance + charges + fees + interest - payments - credits
      = printed new balance
- [ ] Compared with the printed statement, not today's online balance

## 3. Pair transfers
- [ ] Every transfer between your own accounts appears on both statements and
      nets to zero
- [ ] Card payments from checking are recorded as transfers, not expenses

## 4. Receivables and payables
- [ ] Each customer deposit matched to the invoice it pays
- [ ] Receivables aging read as of the last day; anything 90 days past due
      followed up
- [ ] Bills dated in the month entered, paid or not; payables aging checked
      against vendor statements

## 5. Accruals
- [ ] Costs incurred but not yet billed (utilities, contractors) accrued

## 6. Prepaids
- [ ] Payments covering several months spread by month (an annual 2,400.00
      premium is 200.00 a month)

## 7. Payroll from the register
- [ ] Gross wages, employer taxes and withholdings booked from the register,
      not from the net pay leaving the bank
- [ ] Payroll liabilities balance matches what is owed to tax agencies

## 8. Depreciation
- [ ] Monthly depreciation posted for each fixed asset

## 9. Loan interest split
- [ ] Each loan payment split between interest expense and the loan balance
- [ ] Loan balance in the books equals the lender's statement

## 10. Sales tax tie-out
- [ ] Sales tax payable = tax collected on the month's taxable sales - tax
      remitted
- [ ] No sales tax sitting in revenue

## 11. P&L review against last month
- [ ] This month and last month side by side
- [ ] Every line that moved more than you can explain looked into

## 12. Balance sheet scan
- [ ] No negative bank, savings or inventory balances
- [ ] No credit card with a debit balance
- [ ] Uncategorized and suspense accounts at zero

## 13. Lock and send
- [ ] Period closed so a late edit can't change it
- [ ] Profit and loss and balance sheet sent to the owner or accountant
- [ ] Open questions listed with the reports
